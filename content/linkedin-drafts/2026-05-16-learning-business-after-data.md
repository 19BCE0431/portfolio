## Hook

Moving from Data Science into an MBA did not make me leave analysis behind.

It made me realize where analysis is not enough.

## Short Context

The first term exposed me to finance, economics, marketing, strategy, and cases where the answer was rarely clean.

That was the biggest shift: business problems did not always behave like structured technical problems.

## Main Insight

Data helps you see patterns. Business judgment asks what to do when the pattern is incomplete, the incentives are messy, and every option has a cost.

## Crisp Points

- A technical background builds discipline, but business decisions need comfort with ambiguity.
- Cases are useful because they force trade-offs, not perfect answers.
- Planning is not just productivity. It protects learning.
- Product and strategy need evidence plus context.
