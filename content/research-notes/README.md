# Research Notes

Use this folder for weekly research packets before drafting a portfolio article.

Recommended file name:

`YYYY-MM-DD-topic-keyword.md`

Each research note should include:

- Topic candidates considered
- Selected topic and working thesis
- Source links with access dates
- Key facts and the claims they support
- Contradictions or uncertainties
- Possible business, product, marketing, strategy, or consumer behavior lesson
- Personal interpretation notes
- Final decision: continue, hold, or discard

Research notes are internal working files. They should not be published directly.
