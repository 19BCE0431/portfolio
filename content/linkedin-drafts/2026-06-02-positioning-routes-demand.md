## Hook

If your positioning only lives in a brand deck, your search results will rewrite it.

AI summaries will help.

## Short Context

In fragmented buying journeys, positioning no longer shows up in one controlled brand moment.

It gets reconstructed across search, landing pages, pricing, sales, and support.

## Main Insight

That is why I think positioning behaves less like a copywriting exercise and more like a routing system for demand. If the promise drifts across surfaces, the business pays for it upstream and downstream: the wrong buyers self-select in, the right buyers fail to recognize fit, sales gets noisier, support has to repair expectations, and discount pressure rises because the product is being compared on the wrong axis.

## Crisp Points

- **Drift is expensive:** weak positioning does not just hurt recall; it creates low-quality pipeline and preventable support friction.
- **Metadata matters:** in search and AI-assisted discovery, the market often meets your structured product language before it meets your polished manifesto.
- **Positioning should constrain execution:** naming, proof, comparison pages, sales talk tracks, and onboarding should all express the same trade-off.
- **Judge it by operating outcomes:** lead quality, objection clarity, expectation fit, and churn reasons are positioning signals too.
- **Use one hard test:** can the same promise survive a homepage, search snippet, product page, sales deck, and support explanation without changing meaning?
