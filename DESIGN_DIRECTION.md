# Portfolio Design Direction

## Aesthetic Position

The strongest direction for Mohit Sai Krishna is a quiet editorial portfolio: part product-strategy dossier, part cinematic personal profile, part thinking journal. The site should feel composed, analytical, and premium, with a business-school polish and enough technology depth to feel credible without becoming a developer showcase.

This avoids the wrong signals for the profile:

- no startup-bro dopamine palette
- no cyberpunk or hacker visual language
- no excessive gradients
- no novelty 3D
- no award-site interaction for its own sake
- no SaaS landing page rhythm
- no generic founder positioning

The best fit is: Apple-like restraint, editorial hierarchy, slow cinematic pacing, and evidence-led content.

## Research Translation

Apple shows the value of direct headline writing, generous negative space, product-led imagery, and a small number of clear actions. HelloDani uses confident positioning, simple language, soft personality, and work previews that feel approachable. Wall of Portfolios reinforces that product/design profiles need compact proof, role clarity, and trust signals. Awwwards and Minimal Gallery are useful for atmosphere and curation, but the portfolio should borrow their polish, not their runway excess.

## Typography System

Primary type should use a system sans stack so it feels native, fast, and Apple-adjacent:

`-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF Pro Display", "Inter", "Segoe UI", sans-serif`

Editorial contrast should come from scale, weight, line-height, and occasional serif moments rather than decorative fonts. The preferred rhythm:

- hero display: 72-118px desktop, 46-64px mobile, 600 weight, tight but not cramped
- section titles: 32-56px, 560-620 weight
- body copy: 17-20px, relaxed 1.55 line-height
- metadata: 11-13px, uppercase or small caps, letter spacing used sparingly
- evidence numbers: large, tabular, quiet

## Spacing Philosophy

Use an 8px base grid, but think in larger editorial intervals:

- page gutters: 24px mobile, 48px tablet, 72px desktop
- section padding: 96-160px desktop, 72-104px mobile
- content measure: 620-760px for prose, 1120-1240px for page grids
- dense evidence rows can be compact, but hero and narrative sections must breathe

Whitespace should communicate composure. Nothing should feel like it is trying to shout.

## Color And Materials

The palette should be warm monochrome with a restrained strategic accent:

- ink: near black
- graphite: deep charcoal
- porcelain: warm off-white
- mist: pale grey
- brass: low-saturation gold for rare emphasis
- sage: muted analytical green for data/product signals

Avoid blue-purple gradients as a dominant theme. The luxury should come from contrast, restraint, image treatment, and precision.

## Motion Principles

Motion should feel like a premium operating system, not a demo reel:

- short reveal delays, 520-760ms
- opacity and 12-24px vertical lift
- subtle image scale only on entry or hover
- sticky navigation that becomes quieter while scrolling
- no constant motion, no cursor gimmicks, no scroll hijacking
- respect reduced-motion preferences

## Layout Structure

Recommended homepage sequence:

1. cinematic hero with concise strategic positioning
2. profile thesis: data, product, business, judgment
3. evidence ledger with outcomes and metrics
4. project archive with early B.Tech work that can mature into MBA/product/marketing case studies
5. education and credibility band
6. weekly journal for blogs, reading notes, LinkedIn cross-posts, and market observations
7. contact as a quiet final section

The site should behave like a refined dossier: skimmable, intentional, calm, and easy to extend every week without redesigning the whole portfolio.
