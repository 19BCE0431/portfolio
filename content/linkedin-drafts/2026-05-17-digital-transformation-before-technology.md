## Hook

Digital transformation does not start with technology.

It starts with clarity.

## Short Context

In a classroom discussion on digitalization, DBS Bank, and Nubank, the most useful question was not "Which tools should a company adopt?"

It was: what customer friction is the company trying to remove, and what kind of organization must it become to do that well?

## Main Insight

Technology can accelerate a company, but it cannot decide what the company is trying to become. Without purpose, data and AI can scale confusion just as quickly as they scale efficiency.

## Crisp Points

- Transformation needs customer clarity before tool selection.
- Execution matters, but execution without intent becomes activity.
- AI increases the importance of human judgment because weak assumptions scale faster.
- Product teams should begin with friction, not features.
