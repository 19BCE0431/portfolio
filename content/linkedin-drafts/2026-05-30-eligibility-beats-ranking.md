## Hook

If “128 GB” and “128GB” are two different values in your catalog,
your discovery stack is already broken.

## Short Context

We talk about discovery like it’s a ranking problem (search tuning, better relevance, nicer category pages).

But in retail, discovery often fails *upstream* — in product identity, attributes, and taxonomy.

## Main Insight

Discovery is an eligibility game.

When product data is inconsistent, you don’t always rank a bit lower. You sometimes become invisible across filters, feeds, and the surfaces that increasingly decide what gets shown. That’s why “fix search” doesn’t work if the catalog is incoherent: you’re tuning the ordering of a set that’s missing the right items.

## Crisp Points

- **Normalize attribute values:** units, whitespace, casing, and allowed-value lists (so filters don’t split).
- **Fix variant relationships:** treat size/color/pack as variants under a parent, not separate “products.”
- **Make taxonomy intent-led:** one primary category per family; avoid duplicate paths that scatter signals.
- **Treat freshness as a contract:** price/availability/delivery must be reliable; suppress dead-end facets.
- **Measure discovery integrity:** attribute coverage, drift, filter dead-ends, search zero-results, variant fragmentation.
